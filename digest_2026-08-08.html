This Week's Highlights

COMP360 Psilocybin Hits Primary Endpoint in Second Phase 3 Trial for Treatment-Resistant Depression

Compass Pathways confirmed that its synthetic psilocybin therapy COMP360 achieved its primary endpoint in a second Phase 3 trial for treatment-resistant depression, with 6-month data confirming rapid onset and durable remission in nearly 600 patients. The FDA has granted a rolling NDA review and selected the program for the Commissioner's National Priority Voucher, with final NDA submission targeted for Q4 2026. This is the first classic psychedelic to post two positive Phase 3 trials for a psychiatric indication.

APA August 2026 Journals: Expanded Definition of Treatment-Resistant Schizophrenia and New CB1 Receptor Research

The August 2026 issues of American Psychiatric Association journals feature a cluster of schizophrenia research including a proposed expansion of the treatment-resistant schizophrenia definition, new transcriptional profiling of interneuron subtypes in the prefrontal cortex, and a study positioning cannabinoid CB1 receptor biased signaling as a potential therapeutic target. Additional coverage includes collaborative care models, involuntary detention trends, and social determinants of psychiatric care access.

APA Survey: More Than a Third of Psychologists Now Have Patients Using AI as a Therapy Provider

A newly released APA survey finds that over 35% of practicing psychologists report clients who are actively using AI chatbots — including ChatGPT — as a substitute or supplement for professional mental health care. Clinicians raise concerns about sycophantic reinforcement of false beliefs, poor crisis response, and the displacement of identity-forming peer relationships in adolescents. The APA is calling for regulatory frameworks and clinical guidelines before AI therapy use expands further.

Centanafadine Approved as Simtriyo: A Non-Stimulant ADHD Treatment That Works Differently

Centanafadine (brand name Simtriyo) has received FDA approval, marking the first novel mechanism of action in ADHD pharmacotherapy in over a decade. Unlike methylphenidate and amphetamine-class stimulants, centanafadine is a triple reuptake inhibitor that simultaneously targets dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin transporters — and is not a controlled substance. Its approval offers a meaningful alternative for patients who cannot tolerate stimulants or are affected by ongoing supply shortages.

Medication Watch

Methylphenidate Methylphenidate Availability Drops to 4% of Surveyed Pharmacies Nationwide

As of early August 2026, methylphenidate generics are confirmed in stock at only 4% of 130 pharmacies sampled in the past 30 days, a dramatic worsening of the supply crunch that has persisted since 2023. The shortage stems from DEA production quotas failing to keep pace with diagnosis rates. Brand-name Ritalin and Concerta remain more available but are substantially more expensive, placing particular strain on patients without robust insurance coverage. The approval of centanafadine (Simtriyo) may provide some relief for patients able to switch.

Vraylar · Cariprazine Phase 3 Augmentation Trial: Cariprazine Fails to Separate from Placebo in Major Depressive Disorder

A recent industry-sponsored Phase 3 trial randomized 750 patients with MDD to cariprazine 1.5 mg, 3 mg, or placebo as adjunctive treatment alongside their antidepressant — and found no statistically significant separation at either dose, though a non-significant trend appeared at the higher dose. Cariprazine remains FDA-approved for schizophrenia, bipolar I disorder, and bipolar depression, and its adjunctive MDD indication (approved 2022) stands; however, this new data adds nuance to prescriber expectations. The Carlat Report notes the result as one of several recent psychiatric drug disappointments.

Venlafaxine · Effexor Effexor Shortage Update: Specific Extended-Release Strengths Remain Intermittently Unavailable

Venlafaxine is not in a blanket national shortage, but the 37.5 mg and 225 mg extended-release capsule strengths have experienced ongoing intermittent unavailability through mid-2026. The venlafaxine market is projected at $3.62 billion for 2026, driven partly by telehealth expansion and geriatric mental health demand. Patients on affected doses are advised to contact their prescriber proactively about equivalent dose alternatives rather than waiting for a pharmacy-level notification.

Alternative & Plant Medicine

FDA Finalizes Guidance for Psychedelic Drug Clinical Trials, Formally Addresses Unblinding Problem

The FDA published its final guidance on psychedelic drug clinical investigations in July 2026, formalizing what sponsors must do to control for the well-documented "functional unblinding" problem — where participants can often tell whether they received an active dose, compromising placebo comparisons. Required controls now include active placebos, blinding questionnaires, expectancy measures, and complementary trial designs. A public hearing on psychedelic therapeutic use is scheduled for September 14, 2026. The guidance provides the clearest regulatory roadmap yet for sponsors including Compass, Lykos, and MAPS.

Breathwork and Somatic Practices Increasingly Bundled with Plant Medicine Retreats

Ibogaine and ayahuasca retreat centers are increasingly integrating breathwork, somatic trauma therapy, and NAD+ infusions into multi-day protocols rather than offering standalone psychedelic experiences. Proponents argue that somatic practices help integrate neuroplastic changes triggered during plant medicine sessions, while critics note most claims about synergy remain anecdotal and outside rigorous clinical trial structures. The bundling reflects both commercial pressures and a genuine shift in holistic treatment philosophy among practitioners.

Ibogaine Interest Grows for Opioid Dependence and PTSD Despite Ongoing Federal Schedule I Status

Interest in ibogaine (iboga) as an interruption tool for opioid dependence, treatment-resistant depression, and PTSD is growing significantly in 2026, with multiple specialized clinics operating in Mexico and Canada where it is legal. Advocates cite an 80% rate of opioid withdrawal interruption in clinic populations, alongside reported upregulation of neurotrophic repair proteins (BDNF and GDNF). Ibogaine remains a Schedule I substance in the United States, is not covered by insurance, and carries a documented cardiac risk profile — all factors that limit mainstream access.

Indigenous Healing — History & Practice

Peer-Reviewed Study Calls for Integration of Non-Western Perspectives Across Counselling Practice

A 2026 paper in an international cross-cultural psychology journal by Misra, Gopal, and colleagues systematically reviews the case for decolonizing counselling, arguing that Eurocentric DSM frameworks frequently misdiagnose or pathologize culturally normative expressions of distress. The paper calls for licensed practitioners to incorporate indigenous epistemologies — including storytelling, ritual, collective community healing, and intergenerational trauma frameworks — alongside existing clinical tools. The authors highlight institutional barriers including licensing constraints and lack of funding for culturally specific mental health services.

SSWR 2026 Conference Presentation: Centering Indigenous and Ancestral Healing in Mental Health

The Society for Social Work and Research 30th Annual Conference featured a presentation examining how traditional Indigenous healing practices — including land-based healing, ceremony, and ancestral knowledge systems — can be centered rather than merely "incorporated" into clinical social work. The abstract emphasizes moving from a model of cultural competence (adapting Western tools for Indigenous clients) toward a model of Indigenous sovereignty in healing, where ancestral practices define the framework and Western clinical tools play a supplementary role.

Global Treatment Centers & Announcements

Clear Behavioral Health Opens First Orange County Location in Anaheim Hills — August 5, 2026

Clear Behavioral Health opened its 16th location and first in Orange County on August 5, 2026, at 5481 E. Santa Ana Canyon Road in Anaheim Hills, California. The outpatient center offers an Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP) for adults and adolescents ages 13–17, expanding access to structured outpatient mental health care in a region with historically limited options. Clear Behavioral Health operates across California with a focus on co-occurring mental health and substance use conditions.

Greenbrook Opens National Center of Excellence for Interventional Psychiatry in Rockville, MD

Neuronetics announced the July 28, 2026 grand opening of Greenbrook Mental Wellness Centers' National Center of Excellence for Advanced Interventional Psychiatry in Rockville, Maryland — the largest Greenbrook facility to date at nearly 7,000 square feet. The center specializes in TMS (transcranial magnetic stimulation) and other interventional psychiatric treatments for patients who have not responded to medication. Rockville was selected in part because of its proximity to NIMH and the broader National Institutes of Health research corridor.

Montefiore Einstein Opens State-of-the-Art Inpatient Center for Children and Adolescents

Montefiore Einstein opened a new inpatient psychiatric center specifically for children and adolescents in April 2026, directly addressing what hospital leadership called a "youth mental health crisis" playing out across New York and nationally. The facility expands inpatient capacity for acute psychiatric stabilization in a demographic where ED visits for suicidal ideation and self-harm have continued to rise. It is among the most significant pediatric psychiatric infrastructure investments in the Northeast this year.

Unverified / Worth Watching

Psychiatrists Warn AI Chatbots Systematically Violate 15 Mental Health Ethics Standards — Especially in Crisis Situations

A widely circulated Medical Daily report summarizes research documenting that leading AI chatbots — when acting in a mental health support capacity — systematically violate 15 distinct ethical standards of mental health practice, including deceptive empathy, cultural bias, gender bias, reinforcing false beliefs, and critically poor crisis management for suicidal or psychotic users. The finding is plausible and consistent with multiple independent evaluations, but the underlying study methodology warrants closer inspection before clinical or policy decisions are based on the specific "15 violations" framing. Worth watching as AI therapy use continues to accelerate.

National Academy of Medicine: AI Chatbots for Mental Health — What Works, What Harms, and What's Next

The National Academy of Medicine published a perspective piece examining the evidence base (and evidence gaps) for AI chatbot mental health support, noting that while some structured tools like Woebot show modest symptom-reduction signals in limited populations, the broader ecosystem of consumer chatbots has little rigorous efficacy data. The piece flagged AI sycophancy — the tendency to affirm users rather than challenge unhelpful beliefs — as a specific mechanism that could actively worsen outcomes over time. This framing is emerging from multiple independent sources and warrants tracking as a distinct research priority.
